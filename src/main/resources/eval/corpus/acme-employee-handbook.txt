Acme Robotics Employee Handbook (demo document)

1. Working hours and remote work
Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours employees may arrange their schedule freely with their team lead. Full-time employees work 40 hours per week.
Remote work is allowed up to three days per week. Employees must be present in the office on Tuesdays for the weekly planning meeting. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from the department director and are reviewed every six months.
Employees who work from another country for more than 14 days in a calendar year must notify the People team, because tax and insurance rules may apply.
2. Leave and time off
Every full-time employee receives 25 paid vacation days per year. Unused vacation days up to a maximum of 5 may be carried over into the next year and expire on March 31.
Sick leave is paid in full for the first 10 days per year; a medical certificate is required from the third consecutive day of absence.
Parental leave: the primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of fully paid leave, the secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks. Leave may be taken in blocks within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption.
Employees can take up to 2 paid volunteering days per year for registered charities.
3. Business travel and expenses
Daily meal allowance during business travel is EUR 45. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to EUR 140 per night; stays above this limit need approval from the finance team before booking. Flights must be booked in economy class for journeys shorter than 6 hours.
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days after the trip, together with original receipts. Reports submitted later than 60 days are not reimbursed.
The company credit card is issued to employees who travel more than 10 days per year. Personal purchases on the company card are forbidden and must be repaid immediately.
